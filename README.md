## What we are building

Olina is a deep-technology company building source-grounded research and control systems for consequential work.

Our products are designed for teams that need more than a fast answer. Olina helps structure difficult questions, investigate evidence, preserve source context, manage compute, and keep intelligent operations under deliberate human control.

### Product principles

- **Depth over fluency** — important work should survive scrutiny.
- **Evidence before certainty** — sources and uncertainty stay visible.
- **Control by design** — permissions, budgets, and approvals belong inside the system.
- **Compute with purpose** — premium capacity is allocated for sustained research quality.
- **Operational clarity** — usage, actions, and system state should remain observable.

## Platform architecture

```text
Web · Research App · Control Center
                  │
           Olina API Layer
                  │
   Identity · Research · Governance · Billing
                  │
      Supabase · Cloudflare · Creem
                  │
   Observability · Security · Cost Controls
```

### Technology direction

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, modern responsive web interfaces
- **Edge and delivery:** Cloudflare Workers and global edge infrastructure
- **Data and identity:** Supabase and PostgreSQL
- **Payments:** Creem
- **Architecture:** API-first, source-grounded, observable, and security-conscious
- **Product model:** premium USD plans with dedicated research capacity

## Repository standards

Olina repositories aim to keep:

- production and source builds reproducible
- configuration explicit and reviewable
- secrets outside source control
- access and data boundaries deliberate
- user-facing states responsive and accessible
- research outputs inspectable and source-aware

Public repositories may contain frontend demonstrations or early platform components. Product behavior, limits, and interfaces can change as the platform develops.

## Security

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

Report security concerns privately at **security@olina.tech** with:

- the affected service or repository
- clear reproduction steps
- expected and observed behavior
- potential impact
- relevant logs or screenshots with secrets removed
